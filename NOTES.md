Shiftwise keeps your working hours, sites and pay together on your phone. Built for UK hourly and day-rate workers, with no account, adverts or analytics.

- GPS arrival prompts, automatic confirmation using the real arrival time, configurable leaving grace and manual clocking.
- Site schedules, early unpaid time, breaks and live earnings, plus notification controls, widgets and a quick-settings tile.
- Calendar/list history, shift timelines, corrections with reasons and permanent edit history.
- PDF timesheets and CSV exports with period and site totals.
- Optional travel time, saved or GPS mileage, overtime, weekend/night/bank-holiday rates, allowances and CIS.
- Effective-dated government and pay rates, with previews before backdated changes are applied.
- Optional payslip checking, private photo attachments, supervisor sign-off and separate employers.
- PIN and supported phone biometrics, encrypted backups and previewed restore.
- Graphite and amber design, Dark/Light/System themes, larger text support and reduced-motion behaviour.

**Requires Android 10 or later.** Optional features start off until selected during setup or in Settings → Features.

For an update, install over your existing Shiftwise app. Keep a current in-app backup when moving phones. Download the asset **Shiftwise.apk**.

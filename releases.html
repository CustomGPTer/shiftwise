Release notes

What changed in each version of Shiftwise. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.1 24 November 2026Latest

  • Check for updates in Settings. Shiftwise asks GitHub for the newest version only when you tap Check now, shows what's new, and opens the download in your browser.
  • More apps in Settings lists the developer's other free apps by group, each opening its website.
  • About and Settings show the version number from the app itself.

Version 1.0 23 November 2026

  • First public release. Private, offline timesheets with hourly and day rates, site schedules, breaks and live earnings.
  • GPS arrival prompts that confirm your real arrival time after five minutes, a leaving grace period you can change, and manual clocking.
  • A notification while you work, two home-screen widgets and a quick-settings tile.
  • Calendar and list history, a timeline for every shift, corrections with reasons and a permanent edit history, and week copying.
  • PDF timesheets and CSV exports with period and site totals, and an hours chart.
  • Optional travel time, saved or GPS mileage, overtime, weekend, night and regional bank-holiday rates, allowances and CIS.
  • Government and pay rates with the dates they start, and a preview before any backdated change is applied.
  • Optional payslip checks, photo attachments, supervisor sign-off and separate employers.
  • PIN and phone fingerprint or face unlock, password-encrypted backups, daily or weekly copies to a folder you choose, and a previewed restore.
  • Graphite and amber design, dark, light and follow-the-phone themes, larger text support and reduced motion.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.1 is called Shiftwise.apk. It is 54.3 MB (56,987,956 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

1acb1be57abf3f1c14bebe1af96ae4716fc6b665eed5d89c45146a7a6eedd50f

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Shiftwise.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.

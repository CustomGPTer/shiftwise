Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. This policy covers the Shiftwise app for Android (package uk.jj.shiftwise) and this website. Shiftwise is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • There is no account, no adverts, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developer receives nothing from the app.
  • Your shifts, sites, pay and settings stay in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Shiftwise records when you arrive at and leave your sites, not a trail of where you go. A route is kept only for a GPS mileage journey you start.
  • The internet is used only for maps, address searches you make and a bank-holiday refresh you ask for.

Who the app talks to, and when

ServiceWhat it receivesWhen
OpenFreeMapYour IP address and the map area you look atOnly while a site map is open
Your phone's address search (Android Geocoder)The address you type, handled by your phone's own location serviceWhen you search for an address
GOV.UKYour IP address and a request for the bank-holiday listOnly when you ask for a refresh in Pay rules
Google Play services locationHandled on your phone by Android, to tell Shiftwise when you cross a site boundaryWhile automatic clocking is on

Each service handles what it receives under its own policy. There is no Shiftwise server, so nothing is sent to the developer.

Location

Location is used to notice when you arrive at or leave a saved site, which needs Android's location all the time permission. Shiftwise saves the clock events (the times you arrived and left), not your position through the day. During a GPS-tracked mileage journey that you start, the route is saved so the distance can be worked out. You can use Shiftwise without location by clocking in and out yourself.

What stays on your phone

Shifts, sites, pay rules, rates, mileage, signatures, photos you attach and settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for Shiftwise. Uninstalling the app removes all of it, so keep your own backup.

What the app can reach

Internet (for maps, address search and the bank-holiday refresh), location including in the background (for automatic clocking and GPS mileage), notifications (for arrival prompts and the shift in progress), starting after a restart (to keep clocking working), keeping the phone awake briefly (to finish recording a clock event), and fingerprint or face unlock (only if you turn on the app lock). No camera, microphone or contacts access: photos are added through Android's own picker.

Timesheets, exports and backups

PDF timesheets, CSV files and backups are saved or shared only when you choose, to the place you pick. They hold your working records, so keep them private. A backup can be protected with a password (AES-256). Your PIN is never included in a backup.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Shiftwise removes everything it stored. It cannot recall a timesheet, export or backup you have saved or shared elsewhere.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Shiftwise collects nothing from anyone. It is a work tool and is not aimed at children.
